Keep each life’s money in its own ledger

Separate household finances into profiles, switch the active ledger, and share a read-only view when someone should see the numbers without changing them.

Why keep household finances in separate profiles

What you can do next

Separate ledgers

Give each life its own net worth, expenses, and goals instead of colour-coding one spreadsheet.

Switch and open

Tap a profile to open that workspace. The header shows which ledger you are in.

Goals without mixing books

A goal stays on one profile. Goal Manager can still count another profile’s holdings toward it, without merging the two ledgers.

Read-only share

Invite the verified account that matches the profile email. They accept, look, and cannot edit. You can revoke.
