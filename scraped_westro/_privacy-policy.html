Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 28, 2026

Thanks for using Westro. We know privacy matters to you, and it matters to us too. This page walks you through what we collect, why we need it, and how we keep it safe.

Westro is part of finlive.in. Here's our promise: we don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers. We don't hand it over to anyone else. Your financial information stays yours.

Your Consent

By using Westro, you're agreeing to this privacy policy. If something here doesn't sit right with you, we totally understand. But please don't use the app.

What We Collect

Your Account Details

When you sign in with Google, we grab a few basics to keep things running smoothly:

That's it for account stuff. We keep it in a secure database and use it only for your account.

  • Your email address: This is how we identify you and make sure only you can see your data.
  • Your name and profile photo: We show these in the app so it feels a bit more personal.
  • Login activity: We track when you last signed in to help keep your account secure.

Your Financial Information

This is the heart of Westro: the numbers you enter to track your financial life. We save everything you put in so you can access it from anywhere:

We use strong encryption to protect your data. Your financial details are encrypted and cannot be read by our team. While decryption keys exist, they are securely stored in an isolated environment with strict access controls, ensuring that no unauthorized personnel can access your information. These keys are retained by the system only to enable essential functions, such as processing and displaying your data, while remaining protected from direct human access.

  • Your assets: bank balances, investments, property values
  • Your debts: loans, mortgages, credit cards
  • Your cash flow: monthly income and expenses
  • Your goals: trips you're planning, financial milestones you're working toward
  • Your preferences: currency, theme, display settings

Google Drive Backup (Optional)

Want even more control? You can link your Google Drive and keep a personal backup that only you own:

  • We create a folder called 'wealthVision' in your Drive and save copies there
  • Optional trip attachments (itinerary stops and notes) also go into your Drive under wealthVision/trip-files/, not onto Westro file storage
  • We can only create, read, update, and delete files this app creates in that Westro folder (Google drive.file scope). We cannot see the rest of your Drive, Gmail, Photos, or Contacts
  • You can turn this off anytime

How You Use the App

To make Westro better, we look at general patterns, not your personal data, just how the app gets used:

  • Which features are most popular (like net worth tracking or trip planning)
  • How people navigate through the app, so we can improve the layout
  • Anonymous crash reports, so we can fix bugs quickly
  • Basic technical info like browser type and device, so the app works well for everyone

How We Use This Information

Here's what we actually do with the data we collect:

  • Keep the app running and make sure it's you logging in
  • Sync your data across all your devices
  • Set up your Google Drive backup if you enable it
  • Make the app better based on how it's being used
  • Build new features
  • Catch and prevent abuse
  • Send you important updates about the service

Where Your Data Lives

On Your Device

We keep a copy of your plan on your device so the app loads instantly. If you clear your browser data, this local copy gets wiped, but don't worry, it's still safe on our server and will sync back when you log in again.

On Our Server

This is where the magic happens. We save your data on a secure server so you can access it from anywhere and never lose your progress:

Everything saves automatically in the background. No more 'forgot to save' moments.

  • All your financial details: encrypted and locked to your account
  • Your identity info: email and name to make sure it's really you
  • Your latest changes: so all your devices show the same thing
  • Google Drive access token: if you've enabled backup (stored securely)

Our Security Promise

Your eyes only: We can't see your financial numbers. Seriously. The data is tied to your account and encrypted.

Important note: While we do everything we can to keep our servers secure, technology isn't perfect. We strongly recommend using the Google Drive backup feature. That way, you've always got a personal copy you control.

  • Industry-standard protection: We use encryption, isolated access controls and secure authentication to protect your financial data.
  • Isolated by email: Your data is locked to your email address and completely separate from other users.

The Vault Tool

This is for your most sensitive stuff, things you want absolutely nobody else to see:

Write the backup codes down at setup. If you lose both the password and the codes, the vault stays locked. Westro cannot reset a forgotten vault password.

  • Only you have the master password. We don't have it. We can't see what's in your vault.
  • Westro cannot reset a forgotten vault password. Keep your backup codes somewhere safe. If the password and codes are both lost, the vault stays locked.
  • Completely private. What you store here is invisible to us and everyone else.

How We Keep Things Secure

We protect your data with multiple layers of security:

That said, nothing is 100% secure. We use industry best practices, but we can't guarantee absolute security. That's just the reality of technology.

  • Encrypted connections whenever data moves between your device and our server
  • Secure authentication to verify it's really you
  • Encrypted storage on our systems
  • Regular security audits
  • Strict access controls. Only authorized personnel can access our systems

Cookies

Like most apps, we use cookies, little files that help the app remember things about you:

You can turn off cookies in your browser, but some parts of the app might not work right.

  • Keeping you logged in
  • Remembering your settings (like dark mode or your currency)
  • Storing local data to make the app faster
  • Understanding how the app performs

Third-Party Services

Google Services

We use Google for a few things:

Google has its own privacy policy. You might want to check that out to see how they handle data.

  • Google Sign-In: For logging you in. We only access your basic profile (name, email, photo).
  • Google Drive: For optional backups and optional Trip Manager attachments (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC, PDF) stored in your Drive under wealthVision/. We only access files this app creates there.
  • Google Analytics: For anonymous usage stats (if enabled).

Other Services

We might use other third-party services for hosting, error tracking, and performance monitoring. Each has its own privacy policy.

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, share, or transfer your personal or financial data to anyone.

The only times we'd ever share your data:

  • If required by law or a court order
  • To protect our rights, safety, or property
  • To prevent fraud or abuse
  • With your explicit permission

Your Rights and Control

Your data, your rules. Here's what you can do:

  • View everything we have stored about you
  • Ask us to fix any incorrect information
  • Delete your account and all your data anytime
  • Download a copy of your data in standard formats like Excel
  • Turn off Google Drive backups
  • Clear local data from your device while keeping the server copy

Legal Compliance

India

Westro complies with Indian IT regulations, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000:

  • Clear consent: We only collect what you give us.
  • Data minimization: We only ask for what we need.
  • Right to erase: Delete your account, and we delete your data as required by law.
  • Grievance redressal: Got concerns? Reach out to us directly.

International (GDPR & CCPA)

While we focus on Indian users, we also respect global standards like GDPR (Europe) and CCPA (California). Users worldwide have the right to access, correct, and delete their data.

Children's Privacy

Westro isn't intended for kids under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you're a parent and think your child has given us their information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

How Long We Keep Your Data

We only keep your data as long as you're using the app:

  • Server data: Stored while your account is active, so everything syncs properly.
  • Account info: Email, name, and photo. Kept while your account exists.
  • Local browser data: Stays until you clear it or until it syncs from the server.
  • Google Drive backups: Stored in your Drive until you delete them.
  • Trip Drive attachments: File bytes stay in your Google Drive; Westro keeps only attachment metadata needed to list and manage them in the app.
  • Deletion: Request account deletion anytime by contacting us. We'll permanently wipe everything from our servers.

If we decide to shut down permanently during beta, we'll give you at least one week's notice via the app or email. That gives you time to export your data.
